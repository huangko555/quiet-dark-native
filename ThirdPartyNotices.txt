Palette and token-rule provenance

Workbench palette adapted from Panda Theme 1.4.0:
https://github.com/siamak/panda-syntax-vscode
No Panda executable code, images, or extension dependencies are included.

Syntax and semantic token definitions copied from VS Code's bundled
theme-defaults/themes/2026-dark.json and its complete include chain
(dark_modern.json, dark_plus.json, dark_vs.json):
https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/tree/main/extensions/theme-defaults
